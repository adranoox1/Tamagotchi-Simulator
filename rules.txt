
+the capacity of HEALTH / HUNGER / BOREDOM / EXHAUSTION :

HEALTH : 0-100
HUNGER : 0-100
BOREDOM : 0-100
EXHAUSTION : 0-100

+Time left alone :

every action counts as 1 turn, and every turn the pets increase in boredom, hunger and exhaustion
sky : B = +4 / H = +4 / E = +6
land : B = +6 / H = +4 / E = +4
sea : B = +4 / H = +6 / E = +4
health decreases every turn by 10 for each stat (boredom, hunger or exhaustion) that hits 100.
(choosing stats, save, load or quit does not count as a turn, and dead pets no longer change)


+the general (land, sky, sea) rules :

when player feeds pet, hunger lowers by 10, health increases by 5.
(only if the pet is hungry)
when player puts pet to sleep, exhaustion lowers by 10.
(only if the pet is tired)
when player plays with pet, boredom lowers by 10, exhaustion increases by 10, hunger increases by 10.
(only if the pet is bored)
when player heals pet, health increases by 20.
(only if the pet is not at full health)
a pet dies when its health reaches 0, and can no longer eat, play, sleep or be healed.


+the specific rules :

sky:

pets exhaustion increases more than land and sea by 2
(you have to put them to sleep more than you put to sleep the others)

land:

pets boredom increases more than sea and sky by 2
(you have to play with them more than you play with the others)

sea:

pets hunger increases more than land and sky by 2
(you have to feed them more than you usually feed the others)


+random events :

after each turn, there is a 20% chance that one random living pet gets an event:
found a snack : hunger lowers by 10
caught a cold : health lowers by 15
found a new toy : boredom lowers by 10
had a nightmare : exhaustion increases by 10
